Задание 1

Вставьте нужное question word по смыслу предложения.

Задание 2

Задание 3

Это задание не проверяется автоматически — просто напишите свой вариант вопроса.

Задание 4. Чтение

Прочитайте текст и отметьте, верны ли утверждения (True / False).

Every morning, Anna wakes up early and asks herself several questions before school. She wonders where her school bag is, because she always forgets it. She checks what time it is and how long she has before the bus arrives. On the bus, her friend Tom asks her why she looks tired, and Anna explains that she stayed up late reading. When they arrive at school, the teacher asks who finished their homework, and almost everyone raises a hand. After class, Anna asks her teacher which book she should read next for the school project.
